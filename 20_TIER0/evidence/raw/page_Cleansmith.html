#App Store Featured, 3-days free trial / Clean up duplicates & compress oversized media in your album Redesigned compression: size preview & quality presets. As the first and original duplicate cleaner, Cleansmith (formerly Photo Cleaner) is designed to reclaim your valuable storage space by identifying and removing duplicates, similar media, and large files instantly. Our advanced Compression feature further reduces the size of photos and videos with minimal quality loss, helping you save gigabytes of space without sacrificing your precious memories. ◎ Main Features 1) Smart Delete Find and remove unnecessary media with our powerful scanning options: - Auto Search Categories: Our proprietary algorithm automatically detects Duplicates, Similar Photos/Videos, Large Files, Blinking/Closed Eyes, and Low-Resolution media. Start by clearing out duplicates to free up GBs quickly. - Deep Search (NEW): On-device AI analysis catches similar photos and videos that regular scans miss — cropped or edited shots, and videos saved in different quality or formats. - Media Types: Clean up specific types like Screenshots, Selfies, and Bursts. - Albums: Select items from your custom albums to streamline and clear. - Sort & Filter: Cleansmith provides all kinds of sorting and filtering options (Date, File Size, Favorites, Hidden, On Device vs. iCloud), so you can find exactly what you want to delete. 2) Organize Gallery - Keep your gallery neat and clutter-free. Cleansmith helps you quickly sort unsorted photos and videos into specific albums, ensuring every memory is exactly where it belongs. - Sort your custom albums by size, name, or item count to manage your collection at a glance. 3) Photo & Video Compression - Reduce photo and video sizes with our smart compression feature. Perfect for high-storage videos and large photo collections, save up to 95% of space with minimal quality loss, freeing up valuable storage. - Compression feature also preserves the following format: GIF, Live Photo, Burst and Slomo - Shrink Live Photo: Option to remove the video component from Live Photos to convert them into still images and save space. Removing the photo component from Live Photos is also possible. 4) AI Magic Eraser - Remove unwanted people, objects, or background elements from photos with our advanced Magic Eraser, giving your photos a clean, professional look effortlessly. 5) Smart Utilities - Storage Monitor: Check storage of all your albums from the side menu - Home Widgets: Add widgets to monitor album storage - Private Album: Keep your sensitive photos and videos safely locked away in a secure folder. ◎ Privacy-First: 100% Local Cleanup You don't need an internet connection to use Cleansmith because it works 100% offline on your phone. Your privacy is our top priority. ◎ We Value Your Feedback Your experience matters to us! For questions or suggestions, reach out to us on Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/photocleaner If you liked using Cleansmith, please rate us in App Store!
