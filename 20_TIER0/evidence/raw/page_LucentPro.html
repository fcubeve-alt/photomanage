Meet V2 - Try whole solution free for 7 days Lucent is a private AI photo manager that organizes your entire photo library using on-device artificial intelligence. Your photos never leave your device. SMART SEARCH Find any photo instantly with natural language search. Search for "sunset at the beach", "birthday party", or "red car" - Lucent understands what you're looking for. INTELLIGENT COLLECTIONS Automatically organize photos into smart collections: vacations, family gatherings, food adventures, pets, and more. Over 200 AI-generated collections based on your unique library. DUPLICATE DETECTION Find and remove duplicate photos to free up storage space. Lucent identifies exact matches and similar photos. PHOTO MAP See where your memories were made. Browse photos by location on an interactive map. 100% PRIVATE Unlike other AI photo apps, Lucent processes everything on your device using Apple's Neural Engine. No cloud uploads, no external servers, no data collection. Your photos stay yours. FEATURES • Natural language photo search • 200+ smart collections • Duplicate detection • Photo map view • Face & object recognition • Text recognition (OCR) • Voice search • iCloud library support Privacy-first design. No subscription required for core features.
