Clean up iPhone storage with CleanMy®Phone — an AI-powered cleaner app crafted by MacPaw. Gently clear years of photos and videos to make room for new memories. Finding the exact photo you’re looking for amidst gigabytes of random shots, useless videos, similar images, and duplicates may seem impossible. CleanMy®Phone trims unnecessary clutter so you can easily access all your memorable moments. Why people use CleanMy®Phone Many iPhone users face common storage and photo-management problems, and CleanMy®Phone helps solve them quickly and safely: • iPhone storage is almost full • Too many duplicate or similar photos • Large videos take too much space • Thousands of screenshots and random images • Need to organize photos automatically with AI • Want a fast, safe way to clean up their iPhone What can CleanMy®Phone cleaner do? • CLEAN UP YOUR IPHONE STORAGE CleanMy®Phone scans your entire library for blurred photos, old screenshots, large videos, and duplicates — and helps you quickly free up space. • DELETE DUPLICATES AND SIMILAR PHOTOS The app finds duplicate and similar shots, helping you choose the best photo and delete the rest. • MERGE SIMILAR CONTACTS CleanMy®Phone detects duplicate and similar contacts — those with repeated names, numbers, or partial matches — and helps you merge them into a clean, organized address book with one tap. • COMPRESS VIDEOS Compress videos with minimal quality loss to instantly reclaim storage. • TURN LIVE PHOTOS TO STILL IMAGES Convert Live Photos into still images to save space in just one tap. • LET AI ORGANIZE YOUR MESSY LIBRARY AI groups your photos into helpful categories — Travel, People, Pets, Food, Documents, Screenshots, Texts, and more — making your library easier to browse and manage. • TREAT YOUR PHOTOS WITH CARE CleanMy®Phone lets you decide which photos and videos the app removes. If you accidentally delete something, you can always recover it from the Recently Deleted folder on your iPhone. Beyond smart cleanup tools, CleanMy®Phone also focuses on privacy, control, and a smooth experience for every user. What makes CleanMy®Phone different? Your privacy stays on your device • CleanMy®Phone works fully on-device • You can mark photos as Sensitive so they're never included in cleanups • CleanMy®Phone doesn't save or upload your pictures anywhere — you're the only one who can access and see your gallery. SUBSCRIPTION INFO You can download the CleanMy®Phone cleaning app for free. The app has a 1-year subscription with a free trial option that allows unlimited access to all features for three days. Your account will be charged for renewal, per your subscription plan, within 24 hours before the end of the 3-day trial period. You can cancel your free trial at any time within 24 hours before converting your account to an auto-renewable subscription. Please note that any unused portion of a free trial period will be forfeited when purchasing an auto-renewing subscription. You can manage and cancel your subscriptions in Settings after purchase. Going to discontinue my subscription and delete this app.
