Go month-by-month to tackle your library, relive golden moments with our "On This Day" feature, and explore your travels on a beautiful, interactive memory map. This isn't a chore-it's a trip down memory lane. Decide what to keep and what to delete with a simple swipe. Why you'll be obsessed with Swipewipe: - The Famous Swipe: Swipe right to keep, left to delete. Cleaning your photo **organizer** has never been so addictive. - Relive Your Best Days: The "On This Day" feature and home screen widget serve up daily doses of nostalgia, complete with streaks to keep you motivated. - Your Adventures, Mapped: See all the places you've been and the memories you've made on a stunning world map. - AI-Powered Enhancement: Magically improve old, blurry, or low-quality photos. Turn forgotten pictures into crisp, clear keepsakes. - Find the Clutter Fast: Easily spot and remove **similar** photos, blurry shots, and old **screenshots** that are eating up your storage. - Decide Later: Not sure about a photo? Bookmark it and come back to it anytime. No pressure. Your camera roll should be a highlight reel, not a source of stress. Free up gigabytes of space, find your favorite photos again, and finally take control of your digital memories. Ready to fall in love with your camera roll again?   • One of my favorite app finds
    07/28/2023
    Dauwmebdiskdndhs

    This app is honestly pretty amazing, I found it while looking for ways to clear up space on my phone having wayyy too many pictures and found it to be a fun and efficient way to clean up some space and get rid of photos i don't need. The only issue - and why i didn't give it 5 full stars instead of the four i did - was the wait between months to look through as a free user. The app categorizes photos by month, and every time you complete a month it makes you wait a little before going through photos of the next month in time. But the catch is that you only wait a little the first few months you do, each time you finish a month you have to wait an hour longer than you did for the previous one. Waited three hours between April and May? Now wait four between May and June. Seven hours between October and November, well here's an eight hour wait for December! I think it would make sense for it to be a fixed time even if it was like 6 hours or something I'd be more understanding than this, having a camera roll that is years long i'll be having to wait multiple days at some point if I want to keep going which is really difficult, especially because doing the app is actually really fun so it makes me want to clean my camera roll faster. Anyways that's why it's near perfect and hope they will consider changing it to a fixed time, otherwise totally recommend!
